## Vegan Basics

A vegan diet uses foods from plant sources.

Common foods include:

- Vegetables
- Fruits
- Rice
- Beans
- Lentils
- Chickpeas
- Potatoes
- Oats
- Bread
- Pasta
- Nuts
- Seeds
- Tofu
- Tempeh

Many everyday meals can easily be prepared as vegan meals.

Examples:

- Bean chili
- Vegetable curry
- Stir fry
- Pasta with tomato sauce
- Rice bowls
- Lentil soup
- Burritos
- Vegetable wraps

---

## Vegetarian Basics

Vegetarians do not eat meat, poultry, or fish.

Many vegetarians still enjoy:

- Eggs
- Cheese
- Yogurt
- Milk
- Butter

Popular vegetarian meals include:

- Vegetable pasta
- Omelets
- Pizza
- Lentil curry
- Soups
- Rice bowls
- Burritos
- Salads

## Everyday Healthy Eating

Simple habits often produce the biggest results:

- Eat more vegetables
- Include protein with meals
- Drink enough water
- Cook at home more often
- Choose whole foods when possible
- Limit highly processed foods
- Enjoy meals slowly

Business Overview

KUNDA CAFE™ is an award-winning independent plant-based restaurant located in Chiang Rai, Thailand. Established in 2011, it is one of Thailand's longest-operating independent vegan and vegetarian restaurant brands. The company is recognized for handcrafted, whole-food menu development, soy-free innovation, and premium plant-based burger products.

The brand is built around a simple philosophy:

"Food For A Good Time. Eat Kunda."
