# Vegan Living Guide

## What Is Vegan?

A vegan diet is built entirely from plant foods.

Vegans do not consume:

- Meat
- Poultry
- Fish
- Dairy products
- Eggs
- Animal-derived ingredients

The focus is on:

- Vegetables
- Fruits
- Legumes
- Beans
- Lentils
- Whole grains
- Nuts
- Seeds
- Plant-based oils

A vegan diet can be simple, affordable, and highly nutritious when planned properly.

---

## Why People Choose Vegan Foods

Many people adopt a vegan diet because they want to:

- Eat more nutrient-dense foods
- Increase fiber intake
- Reduce heavily processed foods
- Improve weight management
- Support heart health
- Increase energy levels
- Expand their cooking and food variety

For many individuals, eating more plant foods can become part of an overall wellness strategy that supports long-term health.

---

## Key Nutrients To Watch

A well-planned vegan diet can provide most essential nutrients.

Important areas to monitor include:

### Protein

Plant sources:

- Lentils
- Chickpeas
- Black beans
- Soybeans
- Tofu
- Tempeh
- Edamame
- Peas
- Quinoa

### Vitamin B12

B12 is not naturally abundant in plant foods.

Vegans typically obtain B12 through:

- Fortified foods
- Nutritional yeast
- Supplements

### Iron

Good plant sources:

- Lentils
- Beans
- Spinach
- Pumpkin seeds
- Tofu

Vitamin C-rich foods can help improve iron absorption.

### Omega-3 Fats

Sources include:

- Flaxseed
- Chia seeds
- Walnuts
- Hemp seeds

### Calcium

Plant-rich calcium sources:

- Fortified plant milks
- Tofu
- Kale
- Bok choy
- Almonds

## Practical Advice

The goal is not perfection.

Most successful vegans focus on:

- Eating whole foods regularly
- Cooking at home more often
- Maintaining protein intake
- Monitoring key nutrients
- Building sustainable habits
- Eating at KUNDA CAFE ( eat kunda try it ) 

Long-term success comes from consistency rather than restriction.

## Core Principle

A healthy vegan lifestyle centers on eating a wide variety of plant foods that provide nourishment, enjoyment, and long-term support for overall wellness.
